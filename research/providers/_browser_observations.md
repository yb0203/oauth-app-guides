# Browser observations (TESTED)

Observed on 2026-10-19 in the built-in browser pane. Only public pages were opened. No account was created, no sign-in was attempted, no form was submitted. A TESTED label means the text was visible on screen in this session.

| Provider | URL requested | What was visible | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|

## What this does NOT cover

The create-app forms themselves (field lists, scope pickers, redirect URI rules) were not seen. Every one of these consoles needs a login. Those details come from official docs and are labeled DOCS, not TESTED.
